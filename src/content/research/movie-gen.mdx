## Summary

Movie Gen is a collection of foundation models for media generation — video, audio, and image — representing Meta's state-of-the-art in generative media. The paper describes a family of models capable of generating and editing high-quality video with synchronized audio from text prompts.

## My Contribution

I co-led the audio evaluation effort, designing and executing human evaluation campaigns that benchmarked Movie Gen Audio's output quality against competing systems. The audio evaluations covered three categories:

- **Audio quality** — overall quality, naturalness, and professionalness
- **Video alignment** — diegetic sound correctness, synchronization, non-diegetic music mood, and action scoring
- **Text alignment** — precision and recall of generated audio against text prompts
