## Summary

We examine dependability challenges in large-scale ML research infrastructure through an analysis of two large GPU clusters that processed over 4 million jobs and 150 million A100 GPU hours. We find that while large jobs are most vulnerable to failures, smaller jobs make up the majority of affected workloads. We introduce a failure taxonomy, develop models projecting mean time to failure across different GPU scales, and propose metrics to assess the effectiveness of software mitigations.
