## Summary

<Figure src="/diagrams/seamless-m4t.svg" alt="Central SeamlessM4T box with speech and text inputs on the left, speech and text outputs on the right, and four curved arrows showing all translation paths (S2S, S2T, T2S, T2T), labeled 100+ languages" caption="SeamlessM4T unifies all four translation modalities — speech and text, in and out — in a single model." number={1} />

SeamlessM4T is the first single unified model that supports all four translation modalities:

- **Speech-to-speech** translation
- **Speech-to-text** translation
- **Text-to-speech** translation
- **Text-to-text** translation

across 100+ languages, enabling seamless multilingual and multimodal communication.

## Impact

- **450+ citations** since arXiv posting
- Published in **Nature** (2024)
- First model to unify all four translation modalities in a single system
- Major media coverage across AI and technology outlets

## My Contribution

I co-led the human evaluations for the project, designing evaluation protocols and statistical methodology for assessing translation quality across modalities (speech and text) and across a diverse set of languages. This work was essential for establishing that SeamlessM4T achieved state-of-the-art quality and for identifying areas where the model excelled or needed improvement.

## Key Results

- State-of-the-art performance across multiple translation benchmarks
- Rigorous human evaluation confirming quality gains over prior specialized systems
- A single model replacing what previously required four separate systems
