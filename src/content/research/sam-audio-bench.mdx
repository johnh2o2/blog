## Overview

**SAM Audio Bench** is the first in-the-wild audio separation benchmark with human-labeled multimodal prompts. I created this benchmark as part of the [SAM Audio](/research/sam-audio) project to enable standardized evaluation of audio separation systems on real-world mixtures.

The benchmark is publicly available on HuggingFace: [facebook/sam-audio-bench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/sam-audio-bench).

## Novel Evaluation Protocol

SAM Audio Bench includes a novel evaluation protocol for pairwise audio quality assessment:

1. Annotators score sample A on an absolute scale
2. Annotators score sample B on an absolute scale
3. Annotators provide an A/B preference judgment

This two-stage approach — collecting absolute scores before asking for preference — achieves better inter-annotator agreement on pairwise preference than directly asking for comparative judgments (CMOS-style). The absolute scoring phase forces careful listening before the comparative judgment.

## Scalable Evaluation

Combined with [SAM Audio Judge](/research/sam-audio-judge) — a reference-free evaluation metric trained on the human preference data this benchmark generated — SAM Audio Bench enables scalable audio separation evaluation. Other teams can benchmark their systems without running their own human annotation campaigns.
