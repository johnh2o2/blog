## Summary

SAM Audio is a foundation model for general audio separation that supports three prompting modalities — text, visual, and time-span — enabling users to isolate any sound source from a mixture. The model handles speech, music, and general sounds within a single unified architecture.

## My Contributions

### Led All Human Evaluations

I led the entire human evaluation effort for SAM Audio, powering all ablation studies and system comparisons reported in the paper.

### Novel Evaluation Protocol

I developed a novel evaluation protocol for pairwise audio quality assessment: annotators first score sample A and sample B independently on absolute scales, then provide an A/B preference judgment. This two-stage approach achieves better inter-annotator agreement on pairwise preference than directly asking for preference (CMOS-style), because the absolute scoring phase forces careful listening before the comparative judgment.

### SAM Audio Bench

I created [SAM Audio Bench](https://huggingface.co/datasets/facebook/sam-audio-bench) (facebook/sam-audio-bench) — the first in-the-wild audio separation benchmark with human-labeled multimodal prompts. This benchmark enables standardized evaluation of audio separation systems on real-world mixtures.

### Wrote the Evaluation Section

I wrote the evaluation section of the paper, documenting the methodology, experimental design, and results.

### Enabling Downstream Work

The annotation data generated through this evaluation protocol directly enabled the **SAM Audio Judge** paper (Wang et al. 2026) — a reference-free evaluation metric for audio separation trained on the human preference data my protocol produced.
